THE WOOLPACK QUIZ — AUTOMATIC WINNERS

30 questions per game from 1,500-question bank.
15 seconds to answer; 4 seconds correct answer flashes red; 20 seconds break.
After final correct answer: 6 seconds suspense, 6 seconds third place, 6 seconds second place, 6 seconds first place, then full leaderboard appears automatically. Scores stay hidden until the ceremony.

INSTALL: Replace server.js and public/index.html and public/app.js in your existing GitHub repository. Commit and wait for Render to say Live. Start a NEW game to test. Do not replace your admin key.

IMPORTANT: Existing in-progress games may not transition cleanly because saved state is from an earlier version. Reset for a new game.
